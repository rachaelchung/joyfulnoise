What makes our Christian A Cappella group?

Our Mission
To share the truth and joy of the gospel through music.
Our Story

Sharing the Gospel Through Music

Formed in October 1993, Joyful Noise was made for the purpose of glorifying God through music by singing a cappella songs in all styles of music. We are always looking to make a ruckus around Pittsburgh. You can find us sharing the gospel and singing at local nursing homes, local and national a cappella concerts, and with groups from CMU, Pitt, and beyond!

What We Do

How We Sing & Serve

Rehearsal

We are a group of students who are passionate about music, and use it as an expression of our joy in His promises. We perform arrangements from hymns to contemporary music with the same goal to glorify Him!

Ruckus

As a group, we also prioritize what it means to be part of a community in shared faith. We practive music not only for performances but for each other. We grow as musicians and walk with each other in our faith.

Outreach

From retirement homes to campus events to formal concerts each semester, we bring worship and music to as many communities as we can, even nationwide at the annual national Christian A Cappella conference, Break It Down!
